Privacy policy

Novakse attaches great importance to the protection of your personal data. In this privacy policy we explain which personal data we collect, why we use this data, how long we keep it and what rights you have.

Novakse is responsible for the processing of personal data as described in this privacy policy.

2. Which personal data do we process?

When you use our website or contact us, we may process the following personal data, among others:

  • first and last name;
  • e-mail address;
  • telephone number;
  • data you enter yourself in a contact or request form;
  • information about the trip, training or service you are interested in;
  • correspondence by e-mail, telephone or WhatsApp;
  • booking, payment and invoicing data when you purchase a trip or service from us;
  • technical data necessary for the functioning and security of the website, such as IP address and browser data.

We ask you not to send special categories of personal data, such as medical information, via a general contact form unless this is necessary and we specifically ask for it.

3. What do we use your personal data for?

We process personal data for the following purposes:

Contact and requests

When you fill in the contact form or the trip request form, we use your data to:

  • answer your request or question;
  • get in touch with you personally;
  • provide information about our trips, skating lessons or other services;
  • put together a suitable trip or service with you.

This processing is necessary in order to take steps at your request prior to entering into an agreement, or is based on our legitimate interest in answering questions from potential customers.

Bookings and agreements

When you book a trip, skating lesson or other service with Novakse, we process the data needed to:

  • carry out your booking;
  • organise the trip or service;
  • stay in touch with you about its execution;
  • handle payments and invoicing;
  • keep our administration up to date.

This processing is necessary for the performance of the agreement you enter into with Novakse.

Legal obligations

Some data we have to keep because we are legally required to do so, for example data that forms part of our financial administration.

Website security and operation

Technical information may be processed to keep the website functioning properly and securely, to prevent misuse and to resolve technical problems.

4. Who do we share personal data with?

Novakse does not sell your personal data to third parties.

We only share personal data when this is necessary for our services, for the performance of an agreement or when we are legally required to do so.

Depending on the trip or service booked, data may for example be shared with parties involved in carrying out your trip, such as:

  • accommodations;
  • transport providers;
  • local partners or travel organisations;
  • payment and administration service providers;
  • IT, hosting and e-mail service providers.

In doing so, we do not share more personal data than is necessary for the purpose concerned.

When an external party processes personal data on our instructions, we make agreements where necessary about the protection and security of this data.

5. Travelling abroad

Novakse organises, among other things, trips to countries within Europe. Where this is necessary for carrying out your trip, personal data may be shared with accommodations, transport providers or other service providers in the country of destination.

We limit the data provided to what is necessary for carrying out your booking.

6. WhatsApp and social media

You can contact Novakse via WhatsApp. If you choose to do so, your telephone number, profile data and the content of the conversation are processed via WhatsApp.

Our website also contains links to Instagram and Facebook, among others. When you visit these websites, these parties may process personal data themselves. Their own privacy terms apply to the processing by these external platforms.

7. Google Reviews and external websites

Our website may contain links to external websites and services, including Google Reviews.

When you visit such an external website, the provider concerned may process information about your visit. Novakse has no control over the way in which these external parties process personal data.

We therefore advise you to also read the privacy policy of the external service concerned.

8. Cookies

Novakse may use cookies and similar techniques to keep the website functioning properly from a technical point of view and, where applicable, to analyse the use of the website.

Functional cookies that are necessary for the operation of the website may be placed without consent.

For cookies or other techniques that legally require consent, we ask for consent in advance via a cookie banner or a comparable facility.

You can also delete or block cookies via the settings of your internet browser.

9. How long do we keep personal data?

We do not keep personal data longer than is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless we are legally required to keep data for longer.

As a starting point we apply:

  • contact and request data of people who do not become customers: no more than 2 years after the last contact;
  • customer and booking data: for as long as this is necessary for the performance and any follow-up of the agreement;
  • data that forms part of our tax administration: for the retention period prescribed by law;
  • correspondence: for as long as this is reasonably necessary for handling the request, booking or any complaint concerned.

When data is no longer necessary, it is deleted or anonymised.

10. Security of personal data

Novakse takes appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, unwanted disclosure and unauthorised modification.

11. Your privacy rights

Under the GDPR you have various rights regarding your personal data.

You can, among other things, ask us for:

  • access to your personal data;
  • correction of incorrect personal data;
  • deletion of your personal data;
  • restriction of the processing;
  • transfer of your data;
  • objection to certain processing operations;
  • withdrawal of consent given earlier.

Would you like to exercise one of these rights? Then get in touch via schaatsennovakse@outlook.com.

We may ask you for additional information to verify that the request actually comes from you.

12. Submitting a complaint

Do you have a complaint about the way Novakse handles your personal data? Then please contact us first, so that we can look for a solution together.

You also have the right to submit a complaint to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

13. Minors

Our website and services are not intended to collect personal data of minors without the consent of a parent or legal representative.

Do you think we have received personal data of a minor without consent? Then please contact us, so that we can delete this data if necessary.

14. Changes

Novakse may amend this privacy policy when our services, website or the applicable regulations change.

The most recent version is always published on www.novakse.com.

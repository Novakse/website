Why Novakse

Because natural ice is different every day, the route is never quite fixed.

Natural ice can't be planned. That's why every morning we look again at the weather, the ice quality and the group — and adjust the route, the lake or the whole programme accordingly.

Groups of 4 to 12

Small enough for personal attention, large enough for good company along the way.

Joey comes along himself

Years of experience on natural ice, and an eye for what a day really needs.

Three levels

Recreational 1–4 hours, sporty 2–5 hours or fanatic 3–6 hours a day. You set your own pace.

The story behind Novakse

"Being outside, in nature — that's what it's all about for me. Skating on natural ice gives an unmatched sense of freedom."

Joey has been skating since he was a child and built up years of experience on natural ice. He later trained as a sports instructor and gained experience at the 11shop in the Elfstedenhal, working with skating equipment and sharpening.

But in the end, it's no longer about performance for him — it's about experiencing the skating and the nature around it. In the Netherlands, good natural ice is becoming rarer; in Sweden and Finland you can still skate on and on.

Joey Novak

Founder and trip guide

Frequently asked questions

Still have doubts? These come up most often

What if the ice or weather isn't good enough?

Our skating trips always depend on weather conditions and ice quality. We adjust routes and programmes daily, and switch to another suitable lake or offer an alternative programme when needed.

How big are the groups?

A trip runs from 4 participants and takes a maximum of 12. This keeps room for personal attention and guidance on the ice.

How does payment and cancellation work?

All trips require a deposit of 50% of the trip price; you pay the remainder no later than 5 weeks before departure. Cancel more than 6 weeks in advance and you pay 50% of the trip price; after that, 100% applies.

What skating equipment do I need?

You bring your own skating equipment, winter clothing and safety gear. You'll receive a packing list and advice on what you need beforehand. For natural ice we recommend touring skates; ice picks and a helmet are mandatory.

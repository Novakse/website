Skating lessons

Learn to skate better

Your path to skating skill and success on the ice — in small groups, with technique and mindset as the starting point.

Through my own experience, I can often pass on technique by feel and adjust it directly to what someone needs.

Joey has been skating since he was a child and later trained as a sports instructor. He likes working with small groups, so there's room for a tailored approach.

What we offer

Five ways to get better

Group lessons

Train together in a fixed group. From age 15.

Personal Training

With or without a training plan, tailored entirely to you. From age 15.

Private coach

Tailored for a group of friends, family or colleagues.

Natural-ice training

Focused preparation for skating outdoors, on natural ice.

Free trial lesson

Experience it yourself with a free trial session. You discover our approach and together we determine your skating level.

Only when the season is in full swing, during the group trainings.

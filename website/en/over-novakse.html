About Novakse

Hej, I'm Joey Novak

Being outside, in nature, that's what it's all about for me. Skating on natural ice gives an unmatched sense of freedom.

It's not a controlled ice rink, but something you have to read, understand and respect. Every day on natural ice is different — the wind, the light, the conditions. Those sunny, windless days especially are magical. I want people to experience that, because in our society we don't stop to notice it enough.

My background is in skating. I've been skating since I was a child and built up a lot of experience on natural ice over the years. I later trained as a sports instructor and gained a lot of experience at the 11shop in the Elfstedenhal, where I worked with skating equipment and sharpening. But in the end, it's not about performance for me — it's about experiencing the skating and the nature around it.

What Novakse stands for

Recreational skating on natural ice

With Novakse I focus on recreational skating on natural ice. My main focus is skating trips, taking others to places where natural ice can still truly be experienced. In the Netherlands that's becoming rarer, but abroad there are still wonderful locations where you can skate on and on in beautiful surroundings.

I also give personal training and technique training. I like working with small groups, so I can really tailor it to the individual.

Why for myself

Everything I put into it is my own.

I started Novakse because I wanted to build something of my own, instead of working for a company and building it up for them. The energy I put in is never lost, and the responsibility is entirely mine. That feels good.

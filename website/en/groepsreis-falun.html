Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about the Falun group trip

What is included in the Falun group trip?

For €1,295 per person you travel with the group for four days. Included are the flight to Sweden, accommodation, a car, 3x breakfast, 1x campfire lunch, 2x fika, the first evening meal together with the group, 3 days of skating, and guiding and support by Joey and Vera. Not included are your own skating equipment and winter clothing, safety equipment (ice picks and a helmet are mandatory), food and drink outside the meals listed, and insurance.

What does the group trip schedule look like?

The group trip lasts four fixed days, from Thursday 4 to Sunday 7 February 2027: two travel days and two full days, with 3 days of skating in total. Thursday and Sunday are travel days, Friday and Saturday are full days.

Can I travel out or back on a different day?

Other days are possible by arrangement, and we then plan the transport differently.

Can I join the group trip on my own?

You can book from 2 people. Going alone? Then get in touch first.

Is there time for other activities?

Because of the busy schedule, there is little room for alternative activities. That is only possible by arrangement. Would you like to add an activity? You do that in the checkout step, just like with the Falun package trip.

What is the difference from the Falun package trip?

The group trip is guided and has fixed dates, with Joey and Vera as your guides. With the Falun package trip you choose your own arrival day and skate independently.

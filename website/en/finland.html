Skating in Punkaharju, Finland

Where you skate

Finland's largest lake district, and you decide for yourself when to get on the ice.

Punkaharju lies in the middle of Lake Saimaa, the vast lake district in south-eastern Finland. Forest, islands and ice as far as you can see.

On this trip there's no guide along. You get the accommodation and the location, and you plan your own days from there. No fixed departure date, no group to join.

That does ask something of you: you need to be able to judge for yourself whether the ice is safe. If you're unsure about that, feel free to ask Joey beforehand.

Where you sleep

Your own cabin, with sauna

You stay in your own cabin by the lake, with sauna. After a day on the ice, you step straight inside.

The size of the cabin depends on the number of people. When booking, you enter in the calendar how many of you are coming.

Inside the cabin

Kitchen, wood stove and your own sauna

A wooden cabin with its own kitchen, a wood stove in the living room and a sauna.

Evenings by the lake

A chance to see the northern lights over Lake Saimaa

Punkaharju lies far enough north to offer a chance of northern lights on clear winter evenings. It's not a guarantee - it depends on solar activity and a clear sky - but with your own cabin right on the lake, all you have to do is step outside and look up.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about Finland

Is the skating trip to Finland guided?

No, no guide comes along on this trip. You get the accommodation and the place, and you plan the days yourself. No fixed departure date, no group to join. That does mean you have to be able to judge for yourself whether the ice is safe; if you're unsure about that, feel free to ask Joey beforehand.

What does the skating trip to Finland cost?

The price starts at €895 per person for 4 days with two of you. In the busy periods around Christmas and in the second half of February it is a little higher. Included are the flight to Helsinki, the rental car on the spot and the cabin with sauna. In the calendar on this page you pick your own arrival and departure day and see straight away what that period costs.

When can you go to Finland for this skating trip?

The season runs from late December to early March; after that there's no more ice. The minimum stay is 4 days.

What is and isn't included in the trip to Finland?

Included are the flight to Helsinki with hold luggage, the rental car on the spot and the cabin with sauna. Not included are food and drinks, and your own skating equipment and safety gear.

Prefer a different destination?

Besides Finland, you can also skate with Novakse in Orsa, Falun, Luleå and on the Weissensee. All trips are independent as standard, without guidance on the ice. At Orsa and Falun you can add guidance on the ice. Guided group trips are still being planned.

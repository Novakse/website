Where you skate

Skating across a lake where people have worked and lived for centuries.

Falun lies in Dalarna, just like Orsa, on the northern shore of Runn - the large lake of this region. The town owes its name to the copper mining that took place there for centuries - hence the red mine buildings you see from the ice.

What you'll actually skate is never fixed in advance. You skate independently, and natural ice can't be planned: the weather and the ice quality decide each day where you can skate.

You book Falun as a complete package: the flight, the rental car and the accommodation are arranged, and you choose when you go.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about Falun

Is the skating trip to Falun guided?

No, Falun is a package trip you book yourself, without guidance on the ice. The flight, the rental car and the accommodation are included, along with online service beforehand.

What is and isn't included in the trip to Falun?

Included are the flight to Sweden, the rental car on location, the accommodation in the cabins and online service beforehand. Not included are your own skating equipment and winter clothing, mandatory safety gear (ice picks and a helmet), food and drink, and insurance. If you'd rather arrange the flight or the rental car yourself, that amount comes off the price.

What does the skating trip to Falun cost?

The skating trip to Falun costs from €995 per person for 4 days. The travel dates for the 2026/2027 season are being finalised now; once they are confirmed, they'll appear on this page.

How good a skater do I need to be?

You don't have to be a competitive skater, but you do need to be able to skate independently and in control. If you're unsure about your level, Joey will think it through with you beforehand.

How many hours do we skate per day?

That's up to you: you plan your days on the ice yourself.

Is Falun suitable for a first skating trip to Sweden?

Falun is a good first taste of Scandinavian natural ice, precisely because the flight, the rental car and the accommodation are already arranged. You do skate independently, without guidance on the ice: that asks that you can judge for yourself whether the ice is safe. Still, it remains natural ice: good preparation is part of it.

What happens if the ice isn't good enough?

Natural ice can't be guaranteed. What you can skate always depends on the weather and the ice quality. Where necessary you move to another suitable lake or choose an alternative programme.

Which skates and clothing do I need?

You bring your own skating equipment and winter clothing. For natural ice we recommend touring skates; your clothing needs to be suitable for spending a whole day outdoors. Ice picks and a helmet are mandatory.

Can the programme change because of the weather?

Yes. The skating tours always depend on the weather and the quality of the ice. You look again every morning at the conditions and adjust the route, the lake or the programme accordingly. No day is completely fixed in advance.

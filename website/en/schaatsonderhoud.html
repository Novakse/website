Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about skate maintenance

What is the difference between sharpening and skate fitting?

Sharpening makes your skate sharp. Skate fitting makes sure it stands right. Those are two different things, each with its own rate.

What does sharpening my skates cost?

Hand sharpening of touring skates costs €16, rounding & sharpening €27, and a hand-sharpened curve €65.

What does a maintenance check involve?

During a maintenance check the whole skate is gone over: rounding, sharpening and polishing, plus the springs, bumpers, pins, bolts and hinge. If any parts need replacing, you'll hear about it beforehand. A maintenance check costs from €35.

What does skate fitting cost?

Individually, per hour, skate fitting costs €95, together with two people €140, and a follow-up appointment €65. Wedges, springs or other parts are charged separately. For an appointment on location, travel costs are added.

Want your skates checked?

Let us know what skates you have and what you use them for, and you'll hear what's needed.

A sharpening workshop, where you learn to do it yourself, is also an option.
